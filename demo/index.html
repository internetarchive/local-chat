Trail Notes: The Ridgeline Loop

The Ridgeline Loop is a 12-kilometer hiking trail that circles the upper valley, gaining about 600 meters of elevation along the way. Most hikers complete it in four to six hours, depending on how long they linger at the summit overlook.

The trailhead parking lot fills up by 8am on weekends, so an early start is strongly recommended. There is no potable water along the route, so hikers should carry at least two liters per person.

The trail is closed from December through March due to snow and ice on the northern switchbacks. Trekking poles are recommended for the descent, which is steep and loose underfoot in several sections.

Dogs are permitted on-leash. Mountain bikes are not allowed on any part of the loop. The summit overlook offers views of the entire valley and, on clear days, the coastline roughly 40 kilometers to the west.
